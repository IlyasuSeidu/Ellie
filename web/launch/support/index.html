Ryvro Support

Need help with Ryvro?

Email support@getryvro.com and include the details below so support can investigate quickly.

Include These Details

  • The email on your Ryvro account.
  • Your device model.
  • Your iOS or Android version.
  • Your Ryvro app version.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Screenshots if useful.

Common Support Topics

  • Building, editing, importing, or exporting a schedule.
  • Using the AI builder.
  • Changing one-off days and holiday exceptions.
  • Configuring reminders.
  • Managing Ryvro Pro subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Requesting account deletion.
